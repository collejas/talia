Data Deletion Instructions

Effective Date: April 24, 2026

This page explains how users may request deletion of personal data processed by Geoactiv S.A. de C.V. in connection with our digital services, websites, CRM, automation systems, webchat, and WhatsApp-based communication tools.

1. Requesting Deletion

If you want us to delete your personal data, you may submit a request using our contact information. Your request should include enough identifying information for us to locate the relevant records.

2. Information to Include

To help us process your request efficiently, please include:

  • Your full name
  • Your phone number and/or email address associated with the interaction
  • A brief description of the data or interaction you want deleted
  • Any additional details that help identify the record

3. Verification of Request

For security reasons, we may take reasonable steps to verify the identity of the requester before processing any deletion request.

4. Processing Time

We will review and process deletion requests within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal, contractual, tax, accounting, security, fraud prevention, or compliance obligations that may require temporary or partial retention of certain records.

5. Scope of Deletion

When applicable and legally permitted, we will delete or anonymize the requested personal data from active systems. Some limited data may remain in backups, logs, archives, or compliance records for a period required by law, security, or operational necessity.

6. Third-Party Services

If your information has been processed through third-party platforms such as Meta or WhatsApp, deletion from our systems does not automatically delete data retained by those third parties under their own policies and legal obligations.
